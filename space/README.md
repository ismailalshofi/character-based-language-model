A small GRU language model that writes one character at a time, trained on ~2,700 Pakistani business and sports news articles. It runs entirely in your browser: the GRU is re-implemented in plain JavaScript and loads the exported weights (4 MB).

- Model: [Ismailalshofi/char-based-language-model](https://huggingface.co/Ismailalshofi/char-based-language-model)
- Code: [github.com/ismailalshofi/character-based-language-model](https://github.com/ismailalshofi/character-based-language-model)
